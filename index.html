Research Interests

My research investigates the neural mechanisms underlying age-related changes in "cold" cognition (e.g., memory, executive function) and "hot" cognition (e.g., socioemotional behavior) across normal aging and Alzheimer's disease (AD), using multimodal neuroimaging (fMRI, sMRI, PET), machine learning, and longitudinal modeling applied to lab-collected and large open-access/clinical trial datasets, with the goal of enhancing successful aging across the lifespan.
